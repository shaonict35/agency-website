Cookie Policy

Effective Date: November 10, 2026 Last Updated: November 10, 2026

Welcome to Insight Creative Studio. This Cookie Policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies on our website, https://www.insightcreativebd.com/. We use cookies and similar technologies where applicable to support website functionality, improve user experience, understand website traffic, and evaluate the performance of our digital services. This policy explains what cookies are, how they may be used, and how you can manage your preferences.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember certain information about your visit, such as preferences, settings, and interactions.

Cookies may be temporary or persistent:

  • Session Cookies: These are generally removed when you close your browser.
  • Persistent Cookies: These remain on your device for a specified period or until you delete them.

2. Why We Use Cookies

Insight Creative Studio may use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • To support essential website functionality.
  • To remember visitor preferences.
  • To understand how visitors interact with our website.
  • To analyze website traffic and performance.
  • To improve website usability and navigation.
  • To support marketing and advertising activities, where enabled.

3. Types of Cookies We May Use

3.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies may be necessary for basic website functionality, security, navigation, and other essential features. Without these cookies, certain website functions may not operate properly.

3.2 Functional Cookies

Functional cookies help remember visitor preferences, settings, and choices to provide a more personalized browsing experience.

3.3 Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies may help us understand how visitors use our website, including:

  • Which pages receive the most visits.
  • How visitors navigate between pages.
  • How long visitors interact with certain pages.
  • Which devices or browsers are commonly used.

We may use analytics services such as Google Analytics if configured on our website.

3.4 Marketing and Advertising Cookies

Marketing cookies may be used to measure advertising performance, understand campaign interactions, and deliver relevant advertisements. If enabled, these technologies may include services such as Meta Pixel or other advertising platforms.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be placed by third-party services integrated into our website. Depending on the tools we use, these may include:

  • Google Analytics & Google services
  • Meta platforms
  • Embedded video services
  • Social media integrations
  • Other website analytics or marketing tools

Third-party providers manage their own cookies and data processing practices according to their respective policies. We encourage visitors to review the privacy and cookie policies of relevant third-party services.

5. How to Manage Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your web browser settings. Most modern browsers allow you to:

  • View stored cookies.
  • Delete existing cookies.
  • Block certain types of cookies.
  • Block cookies from specific websites.
  • Receive notifications when cookies are stored.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality or user experience. Where required by applicable law, we will provide a cookie consent mechanism for non-essential cookies.

6. Cookie Consent

Where applicable, visitors may be given the option to accept, reject, or customize non-essential cookies through a cookie consent interface. Essential cookies may still be used where necessary for website functionality or security. You may update your cookie preferences through the available consent settings, where supported.

7. Data Privacy and Security

Information collected through cookies may be handled according to our Privacy Policy. We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with website usage. However, internet-based technologies cannot guarantee complete security. For more information about how we collect, use, and protect personal information, please review our Privacy Policy.

8. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in our website functionality, technology, third-party integrations, or applicable legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.
